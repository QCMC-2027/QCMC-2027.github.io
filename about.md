## About QCMC

The International Conference on Quantum Communication, Measurement and Computing (QCMC) is one of the premier conference series in quantum information science and technology. Established in Paris in 1990, QCMC brings together researchers from around the world to share the latest advances across the full breadth of quantum science.

QCMC 2027 is the **17th edition** of the series. Previous conferences have been held across the globe, most recently in Chennai, India (2024).

## Organizing Institutions

QCMC 2027 is locally organized by the **University of Ottawa** (through [NexQT](https://nexqt.ca/)) and the **National Research Council Canada (NRC)**, in cooperation with the QCMC Steering Committee.

## The International Quantum Award

The International Quantum Award is presented at each QCMC conference, recognizing outstanding contributions to quantum science. Recipients are selected by the Steering Committee and honoured at the conference banquet.

## The QCMC Series

For information about the QCMC series, including its scientific scope, Steering Committee, and past conferences, visit [qcmc-conference.org](https://www.qcmc-conference.org/).
