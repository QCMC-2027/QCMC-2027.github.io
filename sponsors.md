QCMC 2027 will bring approximately 300 researchers, students and industry leaders in quantum communication, measurement, computing and sensing to Ottawa from **August 9–13, 2027**. Sponsorship offers companies and institutions working in quantum, photonics and enabling technologies direct visibility with this international community.

## Why Ottawa

Ottawa is one of Canada's leading hubs for photonics and quantum technology. The region is home to one of the country's largest semiconductor and photonics clusters, centred on the Kanata North technology park. It also hosts the National Research Council Canada's quantum and photonics research programs, including the Canadian Photonics Fabrication Centre, and the University of Ottawa's [NexQT](https://nexqt.ca/) institute.

## Sponsorship opportunities

- Exhibitor tables in the CRX lobby
- Advertising on the auditorium screens between sessions
- Sponsor banners in high-traffic areas
- Logo placement in conference materials
- Acknowledgement during the opening remarks

Exhibitor tables are placed where participants gather during breaks, poster sessions and networking. That suits equipment demonstrations, recruiting and in-depth technical conversations.
